SPITI BIKE TRIP – Full Circuit Expedition

Pick & Drop: Delhi - Delhi Duration: 6N - 7D
Overview & Highlights Itinerary Inclusions Exclusions Other Info

Overview & Highlights

Delhi - Shimla - Chitkul - Kalpa - Kaza - Dhankar - Key Monastery - Hikkim - Komie - Langza - Kalpa - Delhi

Spiti Valley, literally meaning "the land between the magnificent India & mesozoist Tibet. This place is the epitome of magic. From beautiful landscape to rich culture heritage - there is nothing you cannot find here! Separated from Lahaul valley by the soaring Kunzum La (4,551m), Spiti valley is a must visit destination in the months of October, November & December. Monasteries to Monesrteries scattered villages, Spiti valley will surely make you forget about any other panorama you've ever seen.

Itinerary

  • Day 0: Departure from Delhi to Shimla.
  • Day 1: Reach Shimla. Drive To The Last Indian Village - Chitkul // Sangla [Distance: Approx 220km, Duration: 9-10 Hours]
  • Day 2: Transfer From Chitkul / Sangla To Kalpa. Overnight Stay at Kalpa [Distance: Approx 70km, Duration: 3-4 Hours]
  • Day 3: Transfer from Kalpa to Tabo via Nako [Distance: 166km, Duration: 7-9 Hours]. Overnight stay at Tabo.
  • Day 4: Visit Dhankar Monastery, Key Monastery, Chicham Bridge [Distance: Approx 125km, Duration: 6-7 Hours]. Overnight stay at Kaza.
  • Day 5: Visit Langza, Komie & Hikkim Village. Overnight stay at Kaza. [Distance: Approx 80km, Duration: 6-7 Hours]
  • Day 6: Departure To Kalpa from Kaza. Overnight Stay At Kalpa. [Distance: 215 km, Duration: 7-8 Hours]
  • Day 7: Depart to Shimla. [Distance: 210km, Duration: 8-9 Hours] Overnight transfer to Delhi.
  • Day 8: Reach Delhi by morning.

Inclusions

  • Volvo transfer from Delhi to Shimla & back.
  • Entire sightseeing as per itinerary. Accommodation for 6 nights – 1 Night at Chitkul, 2 Nights at Kalpa, 1Night in Tabo, 2 nights in a hotel at Kaza.
  • 12 Meals (6 breakfast + 6 dinner)
  • All inner line permits for the trip
  • Driver Night Charge, Toll, Fuel, Parking Charges, etc.
  • Team Captain throughout the trip.
  • An Oxygen Cylinder 24X7 in the car in case of emergency.

Exclusions

  • GST (5%) is applicable extra.
  • Any kind of food or beverages that is not included in the package like alcoholic drinks, mineral water, meals/refreshments/lunches.
  • Any personal expenses like a tip to the drivers, entry to monuments/monasteries, camera/video camera charges, laundry, telephone bills, etc.
  • Any cost arising due to natural calamities like landslides, roadblocks etc.
  • Anything not mentioned in the Inclusions.

Other Info

Must Carry

  • Authentic Government ID Card
  • Woolen socks, caps, fleece jackets or warmers
  • Toiletries, Sunscreen & lip balm, Good UV protection sunglasses

Travel Essentials

  • A rucksack bag & day pack
  • 3-liter water bladder or water bottle

Clothes

  • A sun cap & woolen cap
  • UV protected sunglasses
  • 2 cotton long sleeve and 2 short sleeve t-shirt
