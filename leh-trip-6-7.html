Mesmerizing Leh-Ladakh - 6 Nights / 7 Days Adventure

Destination: Leh-Ladakh, India Duration: 6N - 7D Max Altitude: 18,380 ft
Overview & Highlights Itinerary Inclusions Exclusions Other Info

Overview & Highlights

Delhi - Shimla - Chitkul - Kalpa - Kaza - Dhankar - Key Monastery - Hikkim - Komie - Langza - Kalpa - Delhi

Spiti Valley, literally meaning "the land between the magnificent India & mesozoist Tibet. This place is the epitome of magic. From beautiful landscape to rich culture heritage - there is nothing you cannot find here! Separated from Lahaul valley by the soaring Kunzum La (4,551m), Spiti valley is a must visit destination in the months of October, November & December. Monasteries to Monesrteries scattered villages, Spiti valley will surely make you forget about any other panorama you've ever seen.

Detailed Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in Leh – Acclimatization Day
    ◦ Arrive at Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport (11,562 ft)
    ◦ Transfer to hotel for mandatory rest and acclimatization
    ◦ Optional evening stroll in Leh Market
    ◦ Key Notes: Drink plenty of water, avoid alcohol, and don't exert yourself
    ◦ Overnight: Leh hotel
  • Day 2: Leh Local Sightseeing
    ◦ Visit Gurudwara Pathar Sahib (sacred Sikh shrine)
    ◦ Experience the optical illusion at Magnetic Hill
    ◦ Witness the confluence of Indus & Zanskar Rivers
    ◦ Pay respects at Hall of Fame (Army museum)
    ◦ Sunset visit to Shanti Stupa (panoramic views)
    ◦ Optional: Explore 17th-century Leh Palace
    ◦ Evening market shopping for local handicrafts
    ◦ Overnight: Leh hotel
  • Day 3: Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La
    ◦ Cross Khardung La Pass (18,380 ft - world's highest motorable road)
    ◦ Visit Diskit Monastery with its giant Maitreya Buddha statue
    ◦ Enjoy Bactrian camel ride on Hunder sand dunes
    ◦ Explore the unique cold desert landscape
    ◦ Distance: ~130 km | Duration: 5-6 hours
    ◦ Overnight: Nubra Valley camp/hotel
  • Day 4: Nubra to Turtuk & Thang - Last Indian Villages
    ◦ Full-day excursion to Turtuk Village (opened to tourists in 2010)
    ◦ Explore Balti culture, heritage homes, and apricot orchards
    ◦ Visit Thang Village (India's northernmost settlement)
    ◦ Return to Nubra Valley by evening
    ◦ Distance: ~85 km one way | Duration: 3-4 hours each way
    ◦ Overnight: Nubra Valley camp/hotel
  • Day 5: Nubra to Pangong Tso
    ◦ Scenic drive via Shyok River or Wari La Pass (weather dependent)
    ◦ Arrive at Pangong Lake (14,270 ft) - the iconic blue lake
    ◦ Witness the lake's mesmerizing color changes
    ◦ Free time to explore lakeside
    ◦ Distance: ~200 km | Duration: 6-7 hours
    ◦ Overnight: Pangong Lake camp
  • Day 6: Pangong to Leh via Chang La
    ◦ Cross Chang La Pass (17,586 ft - 3rd highest motorable pass)
    ◦ Visit Hemis or Thiksey Monastery (time permitting)
    ◦ Return to Leh for final shopping and relaxation
    ◦ Distance: ~160 km | Duration: 5-6 hours
    ◦ Overnight: Leh hotel
  • Day 7: Departure from Leh
    ◦ Transfer to Leh Airport for flight home
    ◦ Or begin road journey to Manali/Srinagar
    ◦ Tour concludes with unforgettable Himalayan memories
    ◦ Note: Flights from Leh are weather-dependent

Inclusions

  • All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) during the trip
  • All transfers and sightseeing in non-AC SUVs (Innova/Xylo/Scorpio)
  • Inner line permits for restricted areas
  • Experienced local guide throughout the journey
  • Camel safari in Hunder (double-humped Bactrian camels)
  • Medical oxygen cylinder in vehicle for emergencies
  • All applicable taxes

Exclusions

  • Airfare to/from Leh
  • Any personal expenses like laundry, phone calls, tips
  • Any additional meals not mentioned in itinerary
  • Monument entrance fees and camera charges
  • Travel insurance (highly recommended)
  • Any cost arising due to natural calamities or roadblocks
  • Anything not mentioned under 'Inclusions'

Other Important Information

Must Carry Items

  • Valid government ID (required for permits)
  • Warm clothing (thermal innerwear, fleece jacket, down jacket)
  • Sturdy trekking shoes and comfortable sandals
  • Sunglasses (UV protected), sunscreen (SPF 50+), lip balm
  • Personal medications and basic first aid kit
  • Power bank (electricity can be unreliable)
  • Reusable water bottle (min 2L capacity)

Health & Safety

  • Consult your doctor before traveling to high altitudes
  • Carry Diamox (acetazolamide) for altitude sickness prevention
  • Stay hydrated (3-4 liters of water daily)
  • Avoid alcohol and smoking during acclimatization
  • Move slowly and don't overexert yourself

Best Time to Visit

June to September (road access), July-August for warmest weather
